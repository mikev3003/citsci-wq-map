📋 Guidelines & Thresholds
SANS 241 · DWAF (1996) Vol. 4 · WRC TT 727/17 (2017)
EC — Electrical Conductivity (mS/m)
Drinking water limit (SANS 241)≤ 170 mS/m
Irrigation (WRC TT 727/17, Tier 1): Ideal< 106 mS/m
Acceptable106–213 mS/m
Tolerable213–426 mS/m
Unacceptable> 426 mS/m
1 mS/m = 10 µS/cm. Tier 1 is conservative (leaching fraction 0.1, no rain); site-specific (Tier 2) thresholds are higher. Marine and estuarine water is naturally saline, so freshwater EC guidelines do not apply to it.
pH
Drinking water (SANS 241)5.0 – 9.7
Optimal palatability6.5 – 8.5
Irrigation (DWS)6.5 – 8.4
Low pH corrodes pipes; high pH causes scaling and reduces disinfection effectiveness.
Nitrate-N (mg/L)
Drinking water limit (SANS 241)≤ 11 mg/L
Eutrophication concern> 1 mg/L
Irrigation — ideal< 5 mg/L
Irrigation — monitor5–30 mg/L
Irrigation — risk> 30 mg/L
Exceeding 11 mg/L causes acute health risk; blue baby syndrome in infants.
Phosphate-P (mg/L)
Eutrophication threshold> 0.024 mg/L
Irrigation guidance≤ 2.0 mg/L
Excess phosphate promotes algal blooms and biofilm growth.
E. coli (CFU/mL)
Drinking water (SANS 241)None in 100 mL
Irrigation (WRC TT 727/17, Tier 1): Ideal< 7.7 (< 771 /100 mL)
Acceptable7.7–23 (771–2 314)
Tolerable23–77 (2 314–7 713)
Unacceptable> 77 (> 7 713)
Tier 1 assumes a crop eaten raw and wetted by irrigation. Crops that are not eaten raw, or are not wetted (e.g. drip), are rated Ideal.
DWAF (1996) Vol. 4, tentative: target ≤ 1 per 100 mL; 1–1 000 only for crops not eaten raw; above 1 000 only with treatment and no human contact.
Any E. coli in drinking water indicates faecal contamination. Requires immediate action.
About this test: the kit plates 1 mL of sample and reports CFU/mL, so the smallest positive result is 1 CFU/mL (equivalent to 100 CFU/100 mL). Any colony counted therefore exceeds the SANS 241 limit. A result of 0 only means no colony in the 1 mL tested; it is consistent with true levels up to roughly 300 CFU/100 mL (95% confidence), so it cannot confirm drinking water compliance, which needs a 100 mL test. The kit does resolve the irrigation categories above.
Guideline criteria are applied to the geometric mean of at least six samples over a season (DWAF 1996), so a single reading is indicative only. Low counts are statistically uncertain (5 colonies is roughly 2–12), and counts above the plate's countable range (about 150) are estimates.
Water Temperature (°C)
No fixed limit. Temperature affects aquatic ecosystems, dissolved oxygen, and chemical reactions. Regular monitoring supports climate change impact assessment.
